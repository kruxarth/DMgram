Instagram for your friends,
not the algorithm.

DMGram keeps your friends' stories, posts and DMs. No Reels, no Explore, no suggested posts, no ads. Free and open source for Android.

Download for Android

Free APK · about 1.5 MB · Android 8.0+

What you get

  • Your friends' stories and posts, newest first
  • Your DMs: text, photos, voice clips, reactions
  • Search, profiles and follow requests
  • No tracking: there is no DMGram server, account or analytics

Questions

Will my Instagram account get banned?

It's very unlikely. DMGram shows Instagram's own website, like a phone browser does. It doesn't pretend to be the Instagram app, automate anything, or run in the background. Nobody outside Meta can promise what Instagram will do, though.

Does DMGram collect my data or see my password?

No. There is no DMGram server, account, analytics or tracking. You log in on Instagram's own login page and DMGram never reads what you type.

Is DMGram free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license, so you can check every line.

Which Android versions does DMGram support?

Android 8.0 or newer.

Does DMGram send notifications?

No. An app built on a web page can't receive Instagram's push notifications. You see your unread count when you open the app.
